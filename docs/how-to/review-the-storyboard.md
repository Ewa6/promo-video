# Review and approve the storyboard

The storyboard review is the first of two approval points. Use it to agree on the words and the pictures before anyone records a real voice. Changes are cheap at this stage and cost nothing in ElevenLabs.

## Before you begin

Claude has finished the interview and research and has told you the storyboard is ready.

## What Claude shows you

For each cut, Claude gives you:

- **The script**: the exact text the voice will read. Words in square brackets, such as `[Warm, assured]`, are directions for the voice. They aren't spoken and don't appear in captions.
- **The storyboard image**: one still frame for each line of the script, showing the picture when that line finishes.
- **The sources table**: where each line of the script comes from, such as your website or one of your answers.
- **A draft video**: a lower-quality version with a robotic guide voice. If you don't use a Mac, the draft is silent and its timing is estimated.

The robotic voice is only a placeholder. It shows the timing, not how the final video will sound.

## Review the storyboard

1. Read the script aloud, or listen to the draft. Check that every claim is true and that the tone fits your brand.
2. Check the sources table. Every claim and number should come from a source you trust.
3. Look at the storyboard image. Check that each frame shows what its line talks about.
4. Watch the draft video. Check the order of the scenes and the pace.
5. Note anything you want changed. Be specific, for example "Use 'book a demo' instead of 'get started'," or "Show the reports screen instead of the dashboard."

## Approve or ask for changes

- To ask for changes, describe them in your reply. Claude updates the script and scenes and shows you a new storyboard.
- To approve, reply that you approve the storyboard. Claude then moves on to the voiceover.

## Result

The script is final. The next step is to record the voiceover.

> [!TIP]
> Changing the words after the voice is recorded means recording that cut again. Make wording changes now.
