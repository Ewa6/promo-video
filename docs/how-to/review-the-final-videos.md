# Review and approve the final videos

The final review is the second approval point. Use it to check the finished videos with the real voice before you publish them.

## Before you begin

Claude has imported your voice takes, rendered the videos in full quality, and sent them to you.

## What Claude sends you

- **The MP4 videos** for each cut, at full HD resolution (1920 × 1080 pixels for landscape cuts).
- **A short summary** for each cut: its length, what changed since the storyboard, and anything you still need to do, such as buy a licence or record a missing take.

Before it sends the videos, Claude checks each one for problems, such as a scene that's cut off before its animation finishes, text that overlaps, or a voice that's too loud or too quiet. It fixes what it can first.

## Review the videos

1. Watch each video from start to finish with the sound on.
2. Check that the voice is clear and that nothing sounds clipped or cut off.
3. Check that each picture appears when the voice talks about it.
4. Watch the captioned cuts with the sound off. Check that the captions are readable and spelled correctly, including your product name.
5. Check the end of each video: logo, website, and call to action.

## Approve or ask for changes

- To ask for changes, describe them in your reply. Many fixes, such as giving a scene more time, don't need a new recording.
- To approve, reply that you approve the videos.

## Result

When you approve, the videos are finished. Claude updates the project notes and offers to save the project. For details, see [Continue or save a project](continue-or-save-a-project.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Before you publish, make sure you have the licences you need: Remotion (for companies with more than three employees), your ElevenLabs plan, and any music or footage. For details, see [Permissions and licences](../prerequisites.md#permissions-and-licences).
